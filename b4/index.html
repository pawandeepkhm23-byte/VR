Scenario 1

This is the final part of the study, in which you are given two scenarios. Imagine yourself in the situation and respond accordingly.

Few important concepts:

Experiential purchase virtual experience: a virtual experience that is associated with the purchase, involving an intention to acquire a life experience -- an event or series of events that you personally encounter or live through.

Material purchase virtual experience: a virtual experience that is associated with the purchase, involving an intention to acquire a material possession -- a tangible object that you obtain and keep in your possession.

Please answer Scenario 1.

Scenario 2

Imagine you are going to be meeting a new person who might be important in your life (such as a blind date, or maybe you've been assigned to work on a project together), and you can learn just one thing about this person beforehand, either about their material possessions, or about the experiences they've purchased.
