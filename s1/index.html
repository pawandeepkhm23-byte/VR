Scenario

This is the final part of the study, in which you are given a scenario. Imagine yourself in the situation and respond accordingly.

A few important concepts:

Experiential purchase virtual experience: a virtual experience that is associated with the purchase, involving an intention to acquire a life experience, e.g., a virtual concert or virtual tourism.

Material purchase virtual experience: a virtual experience that is associated with the purchase, involving an intention to acquire a material possession--a tangible object that you obtain and keep in your possession, e.g., clothes or fashion accessories for your avatar in a VR game or virtual try on a product.

Scenario

Now imagine there are two people: one of whom knew about your exploration and reaction to the virtual tourism experience (Person E), and the other knew the same about your virtual product experience (Person M), but neither knew anything else about you.

Which person would better know the real you, your true, essential self?
