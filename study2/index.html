Research Study

Information about the Study

You are invited to participate in a research study examining consumer responses to virtual experiences. Your participation will involve a few demographic details about yourself, following your consent, exposure to virtual experiences, and post-experience responses. Your experience session may be recorded for an evident purpose.

Your participation is voluntary. You may stop participating at any time.

Scenario 1

This is the final part of the study, in which you are given two scenarios. Imagine yourself in the situation and respond accordingly.

Few important concepts:

Experiential purchase virtual experience: a virtual experience that is associated with the purchase, involving an intention to acquire a life experience-- an event or series of events that you personally encounter or live through.

Material purchase virtual experience: a virtual experience that is associated with the purchase, involving an intention to acquire a material possession--a tangible object that you obtain and keep in your possession.

Now imagine there are two people, strangers to you: You know all about experiencing and reaction to virtual tourism of one (Person E) and know all about the virtual product experience of the other (Person M) but knew nothing else about either person. Which person do you think you would know better?
